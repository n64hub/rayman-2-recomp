# for N64Recomp-browser's tools/frame_dump.mjs --soak (through tools/remote_frame_dump.sh, its header says how):
#
#   tools/remote_frame_dump.sh <game dir> Rayman2Recompiled rayman2.n64.us.1.0 <rom> tools/soak_plan.txt <out> --soak 160

# It fails on a Crash: line, the program exiting, the picture standing still for 10 s, or an until unmet (a level the
# game never plays). The steps are tools/capture_window.ps1's, at seconds since the program started, each until moving
# the later ones on by its wait. The title and Start (Enter) to the menu (its language first), a new game through it with A (Space) to
# the intro story, then a change of level as the game's own exits ask for one (tools/survey.js says how): the next
# level's name at 0x800E5D62 and mode 6 at 0x800E5D43, until the level's name at 0x800E5D44 (its letters either case,
# up to its NUL: a shorter name copied over a longer one leaves the rest) and mode 9, playing. The prison ship, A
# through its lines; the Woods of Light, walked and jumped about, and the pause menu with its Load and Options; the
# world map, its prompt to save and the answer; the Fairy Glade, the Marshes of Awakening, the Menhir Hills, the Canopy
# and Whale Bay with random input (W, A, S, D, A and B); the credits to the end. No player has reported a crash or a
# hang in one scene of this game; a scene one does goes into the round. v0.1.18 passed it.
