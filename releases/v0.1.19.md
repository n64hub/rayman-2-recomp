A fix to a crash in a cutscene, and to the frames drawn between the game's own at a high refresh rate. Like v0.1.18 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `50558356b059ad3fbaf5fe95380512b9dceaaf52`).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `Rayman2Recompiled-v0.1.19-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rayman2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rayman2Recompiled-v0.1.19-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rayman2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**Fixed:**

- The game no longer freezes and closes now and then as a scene's fog closes in all the way: in a slow frame the fog could go past its own limit, and the game's own check stopped it. The scene now draws fully fogged instead.
- At Framerate Display, the Woods of Light's opening shot keeps its ground in the frames drawn between the game's own: as the camera shakes, one of them drew most of the level without it.

These come with N64Recomp-browser v0.222.0's renderer and runtime.
