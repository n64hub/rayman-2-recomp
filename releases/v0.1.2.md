Widescreen fixes for the HUD and for sprites at the sides of the window. Like v0.1.1 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `50558356b059ad3fbaf5fe95380512b9dceaaf52`).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `Rayman2Recompiled-v0.1.2-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rayman2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rayman2Recompiled-v0.1.2-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rayman2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**Widescreen:**

- The HUD sits at the edges of a wide window instead of at the corners of a 4:3 screen: Rayman's face, the health and air bars, the lums' and cages' counters and icons, the hints at the right and a race's timer. Dialogue, the intro's story and other text stay in the middle.
- Sprites and particles, such as a lum's glow and the sparkles around Rayman, reach the window's edges. Before, they were cut off on a straight line where a 4:3 screen would end, or not drawn at all past it.

At Aspect Ratio Original the game looks as before.
