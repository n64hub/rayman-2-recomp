Fixes for widescreen and for how the intro and the menus look and move. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `50558356b059ad3fbaf5fe95380512b9dceaaf52`).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `Rayman2Recompiled-v0.1.1-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rayman2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rayman2Recompiled-v0.1.1-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rayman2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**Widescreen:**

- Things at the sides of a wide window no longer pop in and out as the camera turns: the game now decides what to draw for the window's width, not for a 4:3 screen.
- The full-screen fades, the blotches of the scene transition and the pause menu's backdrop cover the whole window, not just its 4:3 middle.

**The intro and the menus:**

- The big title logo in the intro's tunnel is drawn whole, without the thin lines at the joins between its pieces, also while it fades in and out.
- The sky in the intro and the water in the language menu scroll smoothly instead of in small steps.
- The butterfly cursor in the menus moves smoothly, with the rest of the picture.
- A dark fan shape no longer flashes beside Rayman in the prison cell, and Globox's head in the intro is drawn right.

The butterfly's light flickering between two levels is the game's own, the same on a console.
