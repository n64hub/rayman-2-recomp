**Changed:** the renderer and runtime are updated to N64Recomp-browser v0.203.0's. A controller unplugged and plugged in again keeps its port, its player and its rumble; the window's last row of pixels is drawn; and when the renderer falls behind, the game waits for it 30 ms at a time, not 100. The scene transition's fix from v0.1.15 is carried over onto the new renderer.
