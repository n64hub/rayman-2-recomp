Mouse look, and fixes for the HUD's bars in a wide window and for the scene transition. Like v0.1.14 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `50558356b059ad3fbaf5fe95380512b9dceaaf52`).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `Rayman2Recompiled-v0.1.15-windows-x64.zip` and run `Rayman2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Rayman2Recompiled-v0.1.15-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Rayman2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**New:** mouse look. The mouse swings the camera around Rayman, as C-Left and C-Right do, and in first person (C-Up) looks around, up and down the way the stick does. It is the General settings' Mouse Sensitivity, 50% on a new profile; a profile from an earlier version keeps the 0 it had there, which is off, as the game plays on a console.

**Fixed:** in a wide window, the health bar (once it has grown longer, later in the game) and the air bar under water no longer split in two: the part of a bar that is used up was drawn apart from the rest, where a 4:3 screen would have it. Original (4:3) is unchanged.

**Fixed:** the scene transition between levels blotches the old picture out to black every time; it often cut straight to black instead.

**Changed:** the General settings no longer offer Rumble Strength or Gyro Sensitivity: the game has no Rumble Pak support and reads no gyro, so they changed nothing.
